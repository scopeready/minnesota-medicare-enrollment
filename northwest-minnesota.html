Medicare in the region

Northwest Minnesota & the Red River Valley: what shapes the choice here

Northwest Minnesota’s Medicare question is often a border question. Fargo and Grand Forks hospitals serve much of the region, so a plan’s network has to reach across the state line, and Medicare supplements and Original Medicare do that automatically. Medicare Advantage networks here are thinner than in the metro, and UnitedHealthcare and HealthPartners trimmed their county footprints for 2026.

None of the northwestern counties are on the state’s Cost-plan list, so the choice is Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare plus a Minnesota Basic or Extended Basic supplement and a Part D plan.

Cost plans: none of the counties in this region are on the state’s list of Cost-plan counties; the choice is Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with a Minnesota supplement.

Counties: Clay, Beltrami, Becker, Pennington, Polk, Otter Tail (north), Norman, Marshall, Kittson, Roseau, Lake of the Woods.

Questions from Northwest Minnesota

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Sanford or Essentia in Fargo with a Minnesota plan?

With Original Medicare and a Medicare supplement, yes — they take Medicare like any other provider. With a Medicare Advantage plan, only if Fargo providers are in that plan’s network, which we confirm before you enroll.

Are there Cost plans in Northwest Minnesota?

No. None of the northwestern counties are among the 21 where Cost plans are still sold.

How much does your help cost?
